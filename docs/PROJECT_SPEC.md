# 1. Vision

## 1.1 Mission

The Personal Technical Knowledge Platform (PTKP) is a long-term, documentation-first digital platform that captures, organizes, and shares engineering knowledge, enterprise infrastructure experience, technical research, architecture decisions, automation projects, and professional achievements.

Unlike a traditional portfolio website, PTKP is designed to become a continuously evolving technical publication and engineering knowledge base.

The platform emphasizes clarity, simplicity, technical depth, and long-term maintainability.

---

## 1.2 Purpose

The platform serves multiple purposes simultaneously.

- Personal knowledge management
- Public technical documentation
- Engineering journal
- Professional portfolio
- Certification archive
- Project showcase
- Architecture documentation
- Learning notebook
- Download center
- Technical publication

Every piece of content should contribute to one or more of these purposes.

---

## 1.3 Long-Term Vision

The platform should remain useful for at least the next ten years without requiring major architectural redesign.

It should continue to scale as additional technologies, certifications, projects, articles, and engineering experiences are added.

The platform should become the single source of truth for all publicly shared technical knowledge produced by the author.

---

# 2. Core Principles

The following principles are mandatory.

## Documentation First

Knowledge is more valuable than presentation.

Content always takes priority over visual effects.

---

## Simplicity

Interfaces should be intuitive.

Avoid unnecessary complexity.

Every component should have a clear purpose.

---

## Technical Accuracy

Every article should prioritize correctness over popularity.

Examples must be practical and reproducible whenever possible.

---

## Timeless Design

The design should remain visually relevant for many years.

Avoid short-lived UI trends.

Do not redesign simply to follow fashion.

---

## Performance

Every page should load quickly.

Static generation is preferred whenever possible.

Client-side JavaScript should be minimized.

---

## Accessibility

The platform must comply with WCAG 2.2 AA.

Accessibility is considered a primary feature, not an enhancement.

---

## Maintainability

The codebase should remain understandable after many years.

Reusable components are preferred over duplicated implementations.

---

## Searchability

Every technical resource should be discoverable.

Search should cover:

- Articles
- Projects
- Architecture
- Lab Notes
- Downloads
- Certifications

---

## Authenticity

The platform represents real engineering work.

Avoid exaggerated claims.

Avoid artificial marketing language.

Prefer experience over self-promotion.

---

# 3. Product Philosophy

PTKP is not a commercial website.

PTKP is not a landing page.

PTKP is not a SaaS product.

PTKP is not a personal résumé.

PTKP is an engineering knowledge platform.

Visitors should immediately recognize that they are reading the work of an experienced infrastructure engineer who values documentation, technical precision, and practical knowledge sharing.

---

# 4. Target Audience

Primary audience:

- Infrastructure Engineers
- Kubernetes Engineers
- Platform Engineers
- DevOps Engineers
- Cloud Engineers
- Site Reliability Engineers
- Solution Architects
- Systems Engineers
- Students learning enterprise infrastructure

Secondary audience:

- Recruiters
- Hiring Managers
- Technical Interviewers
- Conference organizers
- Professional peers

The platform should always prioritize the needs of the primary audience.

---

# 5. Non-Goals

The platform will not become:

- A commercial product
- A social media clone
- A CMS
- A discussion forum
- A marketing website
- A personal diary
- An online course platform
- A news website

Future enhancements must respect these boundaries.

---

# 6. Success Criteria

The project will be considered successful if it demonstrates the following characteristics:

- Fast and responsive
- Easy to maintain
- Content-focused
- Highly readable
- Searchable
- Accessible
- Technically accurate
- Professional
- Elegant
- Timeless
- Suitable for long-term growth
