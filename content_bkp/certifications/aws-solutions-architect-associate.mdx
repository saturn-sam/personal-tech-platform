AWS Solutions Architect Associate validates cloud architecture fundamentals across compute, storage, networking, identity, resilience, and cost-aware design.

- Resilient and cost-aware architecture choices.
- Network, identity, compute, and storage service boundaries.
- Operational trade-offs for managed services.
- Architecture decisions that remain maintainable over time.
